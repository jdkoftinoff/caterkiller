  Architecture Highlights

  - Double-buffered rendering — two complete sprite engines ($7900/$9300), page flip at $6F52
  - XOR sprite drawing — same call draws and erases (classic Apple II technique)
  - Pixel-perfect collision — AND sprite with screen content during blit
  - BCD scoring — 3-byte score at $8A-$8C (up to 999,999) using 6502 decimal mode
  - Enemy AI — cats compare their position to yours and chase
  - Difficulty scaling — GETTIME ($77AA) makes enemies faster at higher levels; main loop adjusts update ratio
  - Sound mute — Ctrl-S toggles bit 7 of $84 (SOUND)
  - JMP $0803 at $7792 — exits to a loader in low memory for level transitions
